# Revisit the Tracker review experience

Status: deferred by the project owner on November 7, 2026. The current review screen is a usable working version, not the final interaction design. Keep it working while developing the next milestones; revisit the experience before the first usable release.

The owner still finds the review/confirmation experience unsatisfying even after simplifying its terminology. Earlier counters for requirement items and parsing flags exposed internal parser structure and made the purpose of confirmation unclear.

Questions to explore together:

- What should a student see immediately after upload: a progress summary, a few targeted questions, or a guided review?
- How can we distinguish an unreadable detail from a genuinely unmet requirement without making students interpret parser output?
- Should students check only uncertain records first, with the complete extracted audit available separately?
- How should they indicate "looks right," make a correction, or leave something uncertain?
- What should confirmation mean, and where should it lead once course suggestions exist?
- Can we group headings, policies, credit awards and non-course milestones more naturally?
- Which advanced fields and source details should be shown only when requested?

Leave space for sketches and ideas here. Do not assume the current forms, question list, counters, checkbox or button wording are final. Preserve source evidence, correction support, uncertainty, and the distinction between planning inputs and official degree completion in any redesign.

Review this note when connecting recommendations and again before the end-to-end release milestone.
