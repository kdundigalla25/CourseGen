# Versioned curriculum definitions

Milestone 3 supplies partial, sourced rule packs and a cohort-resolution service. Milestone 4 uses supported selectors for [potential course matches](RECOMMENDATIONS.md). Neither service evaluates degree completion or fills missing requirements into an audit. The reviewed Tracker remains the record of reported progress and exceptions.

## Resolution

`POST /api/v1/curricula/resolve` takes the complete `ReviewedAudit` returned by `/audits/review`. It validates the audit and checks that its revision still matches its contents. It returns selected/ambiguous/conflict/unsupported, candidate versions, possible unsupported cohorts, selection evidence, warnings and pinned rule revisions. Student state is not persisted.

- Fall entry identifies that academic cohort. Spring entry belongs to the preceding Fall cohort. A starting calendar year without a semester can cover two cohorts; a consistent catalog label may narrow it.
- A catalog label alone does not replace starting-year evidence. A major's term selects only that major's pack.
- Positive requirement signatures can identify a curriculum profile. Missing requirements do not disprove a profile; sections may be omitted or waived.
- The newer Data Literacy Lab plus Interdisciplinary profile occurs in both verified 2025 and 2026 catalogs. Requirements alone leave those cohorts ambiguous.
- The older Triple-I plus Data Literacy profile cannot prove exactly 2024. Earlier possible cohorts remain unsupported candidates even when only one matching pack is installed.
- A known profile cannot exclude a future cohort whose rules have not been verified. Installing fewer rule packs must never manufacture certainty.
- Incompatible positive profiles or inconsistent known starting/catalog evidence require review. Summer entry is not silently treated as Fall.
- Exact program-name aliases and requirement terms resolve independently; unsupported terms never inherit the latest pack.

A genuinely unique, verified signature can resolve a version without inventing a missing starting year. No currently shipped signature claims that uniqueness. Resolution chooses definitions only; it does not declare that any course satisfies a student's requirement.

## Data contracts and extension

Files live in `data/curricula/` and `data/programs/`. [Gen Ed schema](../data/schemas/gen-ed-rule-pack.schema.json) and [program schema](../data/schemas/program-rule-pack.schema.json) are generated by `backend/export_data_schemas.py`. The loader validates sources, rule references, cycles, cohort editions and duplicate IDs. API discovery is available through `GET /api/v1/curricula`.

Each source records an edition, URL, page/section locator, verification date and content hash. Large downloaded catalogs are research-only material in ignored `data/source-cache/`; runtime loading makes no network calls. JSON rule packs are committed and work offline.

To add a year: verify its publication and applicability, add a scoped pack, identify which requirement signatures are shared with other cohorts, add tests and update coverage. Do not copy a newer pack into an old year without source verification. A display-label alias is scoped to one pack and is not proof of universal policy equivalence. Increment the pack revision when changing sourced definitions.

`kind: course` and `any_of` course-ID lists describe candidate alternatives; attribute selectors are potential matches requiring edition/section context. `all_of`/`any_of` child relationships describe structure. No generic fulfillment interpreter is implemented. Historical course attributes must match their source edition, and section-specific designations must remain attached to the qualifying section.

Manual coverage includes CAA/transfer and placement effects, BA supplemental applicability, program exceptions, pass/fail policies, residency/GPA, language levels, special admission cases, non-course activities, and any selector that has not been sourced confidently. These limits must remain visible when recommendations are added.
